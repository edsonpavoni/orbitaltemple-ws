# Heaven-Based Justifications for Oppression: Historical and Contemporary Examples

Research compiled for the Orbital Temple artist statement revision.

## Purpose

This document contains specific, well-documented historical and contemporary examples where religious concepts about heaven, salvation, and the afterlife were used to justify violence, discrimination, and oppression on Earth.

## SELECTED POWERFUL EXAMPLES

### 1. Papal Bulls and Indigenous Genocide (1452-1493)

**In 1452 was acceptable to enslave and murder indigenous peoples across the Americas, Africa, and Asia because Pope Nicholas V's bull "Dum Diversas" authorized Christians to reduce "Saracens, pagans, and other enemies of Christ" to "perpetual servitude" for the salvation of their souls.**

**Sources:**
- Papal bull "Dum Diversas" (1452) authorized King Afonso V of Portugal to subjugate non-Christians
- Papal bull "Romanus Pontifex" (1455) extended this authority "for the salvation of all"
- Papal bull "Inter Caetera" (1493) gave Spain rights to "bring them to the Catholic faith"
- Known as the "Doctrine of Discovery," this doctrine resulted in genocide and the theft of land from indigenous peoples across the world for over five centuries
- The Vatican only officially repudiated this doctrine in March 2023, but has not rescinded the original papal bulls

### 2. The Curse of Ham and African Slavery (1627-1848)

**In 1627 was acceptable to enslave Black Africans and their descendants because European Christians claimed the biblical "Curse of Ham" meant God had predetermined Black people as a "slave race" who would be purified through captivity in preparation for Judgement Day.**

**Sources:**
- 1627: English slave trade defender wrote "This curse to be a servant was laid, first upon a disobedient sonne Cham [Ham], and wee see to this day, that the Moores, Chams posteritie, are sold like slaves yet."
- 15th century Portuguese historian Gomes Eanes de Zurara wrote enslaved Africans were in such a wretched state "because of the curse which, after the Deluge, Noah laid upon [Ham]"
- 1848: American minister John G. Fee wrote that Ham was made black "by the curse of the Almighty" and "God designed the Negroes to be slaves"
- Pro-slavery Christians argued it was in Africans' interests to be enslaved because "their captivity would hasten their conversion, purifying and redeeming their souls in readiness for Judgement Day"

### 3. Virginia Slavery Laws and Christian Baptism (1667)

**In 1667 was acceptable to keep baptized Christians enslaved in Virginia because colonial law declared "baptisme of slaves doth not exempt them from bondage," explicitly rejecting the Christian tradition that baptized souls deserved freedom.**

**Sources:**
- Virginia General Assembly Act of 1667 legally separated Christian salvation from freedom from bondage
- This formalized what had already been practice since 1619 when first Africans arrived in Jamestown
- The law was passed specifically to prevent enslaved people from claiming freedom through conversion
- This established legal precedent that African bodies could be enslaved regardless of their souls' salvation status

### 4. Witch Burnings and Women's Souls (1626-1631)

**In 1626-1631 was acceptable to burn 900 women alive at the stake in Bamberg, Germany because Church authorities believed witches' bodies could not be resurrected on Judgment Day if burned, and that through suffering for the "greater glory of God" they might find salvation in heaven.**

**Sources:**
- Bamberg witch trials (1626-1631): 1,000 people convicted, 900 burned at the stake
- Approximately 75-80% of those executed for witchcraft in Europe (1450-1750) were women
- Burning alive was reserved for witches and heretics, based on belief that burned bodies could not be resurrected
- The nuns and clergy believed "through suffering and hard work in the laundries for the greater glory of God, they might find salvation in heaven"
- Total European executions: 40,000-50,000 between 1450-1750

### 5. Canadian Residential Schools and Indigenous Children (1883-1996)

**In 1883-1996 was acceptable to forcibly remove 150,000 Indigenous children from their families and subject them to physical, sexual, and psychological abuse in Canadian residential schools because Church and government authorities used images of hell fire showing "Indian people at the bottom" to coerce conversion to Christianity and assimilation.**

**Sources:**
- 150,000 Indigenous children placed in 139 schools over 150 years (most run by Catholic Church)
- "Fear of the judgment of God and the images of the devil and hell were used to coerce children"
- Father Lacombe's instructional ladder showed stairs to heaven with fire at the bottom depicting Indian people; children told they would "end up in the fire if they didn't change their ways"
- Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada (2015) concluded this was "cultural genocide"
- Last residential school closed in 1996

### 6. Magdalene Laundries and "Fallen Women" (1922-1996)

**In 1922-1996 was acceptable to imprison over 10,000 Irish women and girls in forced labor camps called Magdalene Laundries because Catholic nuns believed unwed mothers, sexually abused girls, and "promiscuous" women were sinners who must suffer to earn salvation in heaven.**

**Sources:**
- 10,000+ women admitted 1922-1996 (possibly 300,000 total across all years)
- Girls as young as 11 imprisoned for being sexually abused, pregnant outside marriage, or deemed "promiscuous"
- Conditions: enforced silence, constant surveillance, severe abuse, forced labor
- Religious justification: "through suffering and hard work in the laundries for the greater glory of God, they might find salvation in heaven"
- Last laundry closed October 1996 in Dublin
- Irish government report (2013) found "significant state collusion"

### 7. Caste System and Untouchables (100 BC-Present)

**In India from 100 BC to present day is acceptable to discriminate against, segregate, and commit violence against over 160 million "Untouchables" (Dalits) because Hindu religious texts claim they were born into the lowest caste due to "heinous sins in previous births" and must accept their suffering to achieve better rebirth.**

**Sources:**
- Untouchability religiously legitimated by 100 BC
- Religious justification: "one was born into an Untouchable caste because of the accumulation of heinous sins in previous births"
- "Reincarnation justifies why they suffer for their low birth. They must have earned their suffering through sinful acts in past lives"
- Over 160 million people affected today
- Official violent attacks exceed 10,000 cases per year in recent years
- Despite legal protections, "prejudice, discrimination, segregation, and violence, based on untouchability, is still prevalent across India"

### 8. Death Penalty for Homosexuality (2020s-Present)

**In 2024 was acceptable to stone seven men to death and crucify two others for homosexuality in Yemen because Islamic courts cite religious texts claiming Allah destroyed the people of Lot for sodomy and will deny heaven to those who commit such acts.**

**Sources:**
- January 2024: Houthi court in Yemen sentenced 9 men to death for sodomy - 7 by stoning, 2 by crucifixion
- February 2024: Southern Yemen court sentenced 13 students to death for "spreading homosexuality"
- January 2022: Saudi Arabia decapitated 3 men for "sodomy, same-sex marriage and incitement to paedophilia"
- As of 2023, 12 countries have death penalty for homosexuality: Afghanistan, Brunei, Iran, Mauritania, Nigeria, Pakistan, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, UAE, Yemen, Uganda
- Religious justification: homosexuality is "the actions of the people of Lot" whom "Allah punished with destruction, turning their houses upside down, causing them to be swallowed by the earth, and sending stones down upon them from the sky"
- Taliban judge (2021): "For homosexuals, there can only be two punishments: either stoning, or he must stand behind a wall that will fall down on him"

### 9. Uganda Anti-Homosexuality Law (2023-Present)

**In 2023 was acceptable in Uganda to sentence people to death for "aggravated homosexuality" because American evangelical Christians spent decades promoting the belief that homosexuality corrupts souls and denies salvation, framing it as a Western attempt to recruit African children away from God.**

**Sources:**
- Anti-Homosexuality Act signed May 26, 2023 by President Museveni
- Prescribes death penalty for "aggravated homosexuality" (repeat offenses, positions of authority, etc.)
- Deep links to American evangelical groups who headlined 2009 Kampala conference "exposing the homosexuals' agenda"
- American evangelicals promoted that gay Westerners are "corrupting and recruiting children around the world" away from salvation
- 2009 "kill the gays" bill introduced after conference featuring anti-gay evangelical Scott Lively
- Homosexuality framed as Western import that threatens "traditional" (Christian) Ugandan values and eternal salvation

### 10. Taliban Education Ban on Afghan Women (2021-Present)

**In 2021-2025 is acceptable to deny all education to 20 million Afghan women and girls because Taliban leader Haibatullah Akhundzada imposed his interpretation that educated women violate Islamic principles, despite admitting in January 2025 that "this is not in Islamic law, but our personal choice."**

**Sources:**
- September 2021: Only boys permitted to return to school after Taliban takeover
- March 2022: Taliban promised to reopen girls' schools but broke this promise
- December 2022: Ban extended to women in universities
- Taliban provides no specific Islamic justification despite claiming alignment with Islamic law
- Multiple Islamic scholars confirm: "In Islam, women, like men, are obligated to pursue knowledge. In the Quran, Allah orders both sexes to increase their knowledge"
- January 2025: Senior Taliban deputy admitted "We are committing an injustice against 20 million people out of a population of 40 million, depriving them of all their rights. This is not in Islamic law, but our personal choice or nature"
- Affects 20 million women and girls (half of Afghanistan's population)

## ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTED EXAMPLES

### Spanish Inquisition and Forced Conversions (1478-1834)

**Timeline:**
- November 1, 1478: Pope Sixtus IV authorized Spanish monarchs to name inquisitors
- March 31, 1492: Edict giving Spanish Jews choice of exile or baptism - 160,000+ expelled
- 1502: Islam proscribed; Muslims faced conversion or death
- Duration: Over 200 years of persecution

**Impact:**
- 32,000 executions over 200+ years
- 2,000 burned at the stake during Torquemada's tenure alone
- Hundreds of thousands of forced conversions
- Targeting of conversos (Jewish converts) and Moriscos (Muslim converts)

**Religious Justification:**
- Identify "heretics" among converts
- Save souls through forced conversion or death
- Auto-da-fé ceremonies presented executions as acts of faith

### Limpieza de Sangre - Blood Purity Laws (1449-1600s)

**Key Facts:**
- 1449: Toledo anti-converso revolt created "Sentencia-Estatuto"
- Deemed "first example in history of legalized racism"
- By 1500: Most Spanish organizations required "blood purity" for membership
- Continued into 17th century

**Religious Justification:**
- Alleged Jewish role in killing Christ was "original sin inherited in the blood"
- Claimed baptism could not purge conversos of this crime
- Despite being Christian converts, Jewish ancestry meant they were spiritually tainted
- Excluded from guilds, religious orders, colleges, certain towns

### Iranian Stoning for Adultery (2002-Present)

**Current Status:**
- Official moratorium since 2002, but stoning remains valid under Sharia in Penal Code
- At least 2 women sentenced to stoning since 2015
- Three death sentences by stoning in 2017
- January 2023: Iranian politician defended stoning as "good Islamic law" and "very nice deterrent"

**Religious Elements:**
- Based on Hadith (not Quran)
- Gendered implementation: women buried deeper than men, making escape impossible
- Justified as deterrent and religious obligation

## ANALYSIS: PATTERNS ACROSS EXAMPLES

### Common Theological Mechanisms

1. **Original/Inherited Sin** - Claims that certain groups carry inherent spiritual defects (Curse of Ham, Limpieza de sangre, caste karma)

2. **Beneficial Suffering** - Argument that earthly oppression serves victims' spiritual interests (slavery "hastening conversion," Magdalene laundries earning salvation, residential school "saving souls")

3. **Divine Command** - Direct claims that God/Allah/religious texts mandate the oppression (Papal bulls, sodomy death penalties, witch burnings)

4. **Spiritual Contamination** - Belief that oppressed groups threaten others' salvation (Uganda anti-gay laws, blood purity, untouchability)

5. **Salvation Through Submission** - Teaching that accepting oppression is path to heaven (caste acceptance of status, women's obedience, enslaved people's submission)

### Historical to Contemporary Continuum

**Medieval/Colonial (1400s-1700s):**
- Papal bulls authorizing conquest and slavery
- Witch burnings and Inquisition
- Colonial slavery justified by "Curse of Ham"
- Limpieza de sangre

**Modern Era (1800s-1900s):**
- Continued slavery justifications
- Residential schools
- Magdalene laundries
- Caste discrimination

**Contemporary (2000s-2025):**
- Death penalties for homosexuality (multiple countries)
- Taliban education bans
- Continued caste violence
- Uganda anti-gay laws influenced by evangelicals
- Iranian stoning

### Geographic and Religious Diversity

**Christianity:**
- Catholic: Papal bulls, Inquisition, Magdalene laundries, residential schools

**Islam:**
